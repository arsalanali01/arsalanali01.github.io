Building a useful, grounded quality knowledge assistant

Worked on quality knowledge organization and retrieval so engineers can find relevant investigation history, procedures, and lessons learned.

The engineering problem

Search and AI assistance are only useful when the underlying knowledge is organized, relevant, current, and accessible to the right person.

My contribution

  • Developed a SharePoint-centered knowledge repository concept, taxonomy, and assistant prototype using Microsoft Copilot tools.
  • Explored search and summarization across investigation records, master files, procedures, and supporting documents.
  • Advanced an isolated Azure retrieval foundation with search indexing, embeddings, and storage integration.
  • Validated application identity access and investigated authorization and configuration gaps before activation.
  • Separated deployed components, disabled ingestion, and remaining release requirements in technical handoff work.

Outcome & current scope

Developed repository and assistant prototypes plus an isolated Azure retrieval foundation. Permission-aware activation, ingestion, and release validation remain staged work.

What this taught me

Retrieval quality, access control, and evaluation are product requirements, not final configuration details.
