IDEAStudio Makerspace / Leadership

Helping students move from CAD to a physical part

Supported a fabrication environment as an instructor and technician, combining equipment care with hands-on design guidance.

The engineering problem

A makerspace serves people with very different experience levels. Safe, repeatable workflows and clear instruction are essential to both learning and equipment reliability.

My contribution

  • Trained students on 3D printers, laser cutters, woodshop tools, and CNC equipment.
  • Guided design-to-part workflows, including fabrication constraints and practical setup decisions.
  • Performed calibration, troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance.
  • Developed a Python inventory-tracking tool to improve material visibility.

Outcome & current scope

Trained 200+ students on fabrication tools and supported a space used by 80+ students weekly.

What this taught me

Technical communication is a practical engineering skill: the right explanation changes with the learner and the task.
