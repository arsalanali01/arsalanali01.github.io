Independent training project / Quality systems

Applying DMAIC from baseline to control plan

Used a simulated employee-arrival process to practice a complete Six Sigma improvement investigation with statistical and root-cause tools.

Context
Independent training project
Period
May–June 2025
Project status
Completed simulation
Conceptual illustration

The engineering problem

The training dataset represented a low on-time arrival rate. The task was to define the problem, understand variation, identify causes, and propose sustainable controls.

My contribution

  • Analyzed a 300-sample dataset in Minitab.
  • Applied Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, and Control to the simulated process.
  • Used fishbone diagrams, 5 Whys, Pareto analysis, and correlation analysis to investigate potential causes.
  • Proposed monitoring, scheduling communication, and a control plan.

Outcome & current scope

In the simulated training dataset, reported on-time arrival improved from 10% to over 90%, with a sigma-level change from 0.2 to 2.7. These are simulation results.

What this taught me

A numerical improvement is incomplete without an explanation of the cause and a plan to monitor the process.
