The engineering problem

A calculation or measurement needs clear assumptions, units, uncertainty, and a defensible link to the physical system.

My contribution

  • Handled truss-force hand calculations for a solid-mechanics team project.
  • Worked with free-body diagrams, equilibrium, internal forces, and structural reasoning.
  • Used LabVIEW and DAQ workflows in measurement labs involving acceleration, electromechanical, and thermal measurements.
  • Practiced statistical summaries, confidence intervals, and uncertainty analysis in lab reporting.

Outcome & current scope

Produced course calculations, laboratory analyses, and technical reports with explicit assumptions, units, and measurement uncertainty.

What this taught me

Good engineering analysis makes the boundary, assumptions, units, and limits of the result easy to inspect.
