Reducing false failures at the test fixture

Used mechanical design and iterative fixture revisions to improve electrical-test repeatability on PCB assemblies.

Context
PPSI
Period
2025
Project status
Completed internship work
Conceptual illustration

The engineering problem

An electrical-test failure can come from a defective product or from poor fixture contact and alignment. Separating those causes prevents unnecessary rework and repeated testing.

My contribution

  • Designed three 3D-printed fixture revisions in SolidWorks for the Takaya APT-9600 flying-probe workflow.
  • Worked through board support, component clearance, alignment, and repeatable placement across three board families.
  • Supported testing and troubleshooting alongside engineering and production personnel.
  • Used electrical findings and physical fit observations to guide the next mechanical revision.

Outcome & current scope

Three 3D-printed fixture revisions reduced electrical false failures by 30% across three board families. The improvement concerns test repeatability and false failures.

What this taught me

Mechanical interfaces can determine the reliability of an electrical measurement. Test engineering benefits from both disciplines.
