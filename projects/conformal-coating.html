PPSI / Manufacturing

Protecting sensitive components during coating

Designed a reusable protective cover to support conformal-coating work on assemblies with sensitive LCD components.

Context
PPSI
Period
2025
Project status
Designed & prototyped
Conceptual illustration

The engineering problem

A protective tool must fit the assembly, shield the intended surfaces, and remain practical for the operator handling the coating process.

My contribution

  • Modeled a component-specific cover using SolidWorks and component dimensions.
  • Applied fit, clearance, and manufacturing considerations to the cover geometry.
  • Prototyped the design with 3D-printing and slicing workflows.
  • Considered ESD handling and repeatable use in the production process.

Outcome & current scope

Produced CAD and prototype work for a reusable cover supporting a sensitive-component coating operation.

What this taught me

A small piece of tooling can improve consistency when it is designed around the operator's actual task.
