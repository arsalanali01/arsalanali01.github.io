Practicing industrial sequences and fault logic

Practiced industrial control logic through simulated conveyor sorting and batch fill-and-seal sequences.

The engineering problem

A sequence needs to handle sensor conditions, interlocks, transitions, and faults rather than simply turn outputs on in order.

My contribution

  • Developed ladder-logic routines for simulated conveyor sorting, motor operation, and sensor interlocks.
  • Explored a structured-text batch sequence with explicit states.
  • Used timers and conditions to manage fill-and-seal transitions.
  • Considered alarm and fault-handling behavior.

Outcome & current scope

Developed conveyor and batch-sequence simulations to practice interlocks, state transitions, timers, and faults.

What this taught me

Explicit states and safe transitions make control behavior easier to understand and troubleshoot.
