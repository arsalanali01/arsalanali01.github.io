Keeping the complete investigation story together

Designed a reusable master-file structure for investigation background, analysis, test findings, supplier communication, actions, and conclusions.

The engineering problem

A project becomes difficult to continue or review when its narrative and evidence live in separate files with no consistent structure.

My contribution

  • Built a template that collects project context, warranty analysis, returned-part information, testing, supplier findings, timelines, and actions.
  • Structured sections so engineers can update the record as an investigation progresses.
  • Connected the documentation approach with 8D thinking, closure evidence, and lessons learned.
  • Explored report-building and data-population concepts as a next step beyond the template.

Outcome & current scope

Created a reusable master-file template and documentation structure. Report-builder automation remained a follow-on concept.

What this taught me

Standardization should make an investigation easier to understand without forcing every issue into the same technical narrative.
